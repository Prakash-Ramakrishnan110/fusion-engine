# Startup India & MSME Standard Employment, Contractor & Legal Templates
## Fusion Engine Technology — Legal & Operational Repository

This document contains standardized legal and operational templates aligned with **Startup India (DPIIT)** and **MSME (Government of India)** guidelines for hiring developers, contractors, and sales telecallers.

---

### Template 1: Independent Contractor & Milestone-Based Freelance Agreement
*For Software Developers, UI/UX Designers, and QA Engineers*

```text
INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT
(Milestone & Deliverable-Based)

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between:

FUSION ENGINE TECHNOLOGY ("Company"), a recognized enterprise under Udyam MSME (UDYAM-TN-04-0044558) having its principal place of business at Palacode, Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu - 636808, India;
AND
[Contractor Name], residing at [Contractor Address], PAN: [PAN Number] ("Contractor").

1. ENGAGEMENT AND SERVICES
Company hereby engages Contractor, and Contractor agrees to perform software development services for [Project Name / Restaurant Management SaaS] in accordance with the specifications in Schedule A.

2. MILESTONE COMPENSATION AND ACCEPTANCE
Compensation shall be strictly tied to approved deliverables as set forth below:
- Milestone 1: [Deliverable Description, e.g., Approved Architecture & Schema] — INR [Amount]
- Milestone 2: [Deliverable Description, e.g., Backend APIs & Database Integration] — INR [Amount]
- Milestone 3: [Deliverable Description, e.g., Frontend & Core Workflows] — INR [Amount]
- Milestone 4: [Deliverable Description, e.g., Testing, Bug Fixes & Handover] — INR [Amount]

Payment Terms: Upon completion of each milestone, Contractor shall submit deliverables, test proofs, and repository commits. Company shall verify within 5 business days. Once marked 'Approved', payment will be disbursed within 3-5 business days via Bank Transfer.

3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS
Contractor acknowledges they are an independent contractor and not an employee. Contractor is responsible for all applicable taxes and statutory withholdings.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in all code, documentation, designs, algorithms, and inventions developed under this Agreement. Work shall be deemed "Work Made for Hire."

5. NON-DISCLOSURE & CONFIDENTIALITY
Contractor agrees to hold all proprietary code, database credentials, client data, and business strategies in strict confidence for a period of 3 years following termination.

6. TERMINATION AND DEFECT CORRECTION
Company reserves the right to request revisions if deliverables do not satisfy agreed acceptance criteria. Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice. Payment shall only be due for verified and accepted milestones.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement:
For Fusion Engine Technology: _____________________    Contractor Signature: _____________________
```

### Template 2: Telecaller & Sales Partner Engagement Agreement
*For Telecallers, Lead Generation Executives, and Sales Affiliates*

```text
TELECALLER LEAD ENGAGEMENT & COMPENSATION POLICY

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
Fusion Engine Technology engages Telecaller to generate qualified sales prospects and restaurant management SaaS demonstrations.

2. COMPENSATION MODELS
- Model A (Qualified Lead): INR 150 per Accepted Qualified Lead.
- Model B (Conversion Bonus): INR [Amount] / [%] per paying restaurant onboarded.
- Model C: Base Retainer + Incentive (as mutually specified in Annexure 1).

3. LEAD QUALIFICATION CRITERIA
A lead is deemed 'Qualified' and eligible for payout ONLY when:
a) The prospect is a verified, operating food & beverage establishment (restaurant, cafe, bar).
b) Contact person is the Owner, Manager, or decision-maker with verifiable phone/email.
c) Telecaller records call notes, interest confirmation, and scheduling of product demo.
d) Lead is NOT an existing duplicate, inactive number, or fraudulent submission.

4. DEDUCTIONS & AUDIT REVERSAL
Any lead discovered to be fabricated, duplicate, or unverified shall be marked 'Rejected' with zero payout. Intentional submission of false contacts shall result in immediate contract cancellation.

5. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Leads are reviewed weekly. Verified lead incentives are processed bi-weekly or monthly directly to Telecaller's registered UPI/Bank account.
```

### Template 3: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) & Proprietary Information Undertaking

```text
NON-DISCLOSURE & PROPRIETARY INFORMATION UNDERTAKING

I, [Full Name], in consideration of my association with Fusion Engine Technology, agree to:
1. Hold all proprietary knowledge, customer databases, technical architectures, secret API keys, algorithms, and SaaS product blueprints in strict confidence.
2. Not replicate, reverse-engineer, share, or disclose any proprietary assets to any third party without prior written consent from the Founder.
3. Upon disengagement, immediately delete and return all local repository clones, credentials, database backups, and documentation.
4. Recognize that any breach may result in immediate legal remedies under the Indian Information Technology Act (2000) and Indian Contract Act (1872).
```

### Template 4: Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment Agreement

```text
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

1. Complete Assignment: The Developer/Contributor hereby unconditionally assigns, transfers, and conveys to Fusion Engine Technology all worldwide rights, titles, copyrights, trade secrets, patents, and intellectual property rights in any code, UI designs, databases, and assets created during the project tenure.
2. Moral Rights Waiver: To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Contributor irrevocably waives all moral rights in the work.
3. No Third-Party Infringement: The Contributor warrants that all code provided is original or uses permitted open-source licenses compatible with commercial proprietary software.
```
